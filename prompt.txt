Build the strongest chess engine in 4 KB of Python for the make.fun bounty tiny-chess-01 (Build the strongest chess engine in 4 KB of Python, $500, top 20 paid). Write a Python chess engine as readable source, then pack it into main.py of at most 4096 bytes with the kit's compress command. The engine must play strong chess from any position: tapered PeSTO piece-square evaluation, alpha-beta / PVS search with iterative deepening, quiescence search, transposition table, MVV-LVA captures-first plus killer/history move ordering, and time management that uses the available clock without overrunning. The dev phase grades on fixed practice openings but the final phase uses unseen openings, so build genuine playing strength — do not rely on an opening book for the practice games; a small book is only a bonus. Run the kit's grade-local on the practice trials, analyze the games, revise the engine, and re-run until the local Elo is comfortably above the 1154 final floor — target at least 1400. Keep the repository clean: LICENSE, README.md with make.fun badges and a link to the bounty, readable engine source, and main.py as the packed entrypoint.
